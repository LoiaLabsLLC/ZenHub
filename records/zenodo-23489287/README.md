## Description

Working note on the asymptotics of the normalized centered inverse-binomial moment R(m,n) = 4^m A_m(n)/n^(2m), building on the author's paper "Centered inverse-binomial moments" (Zenodo). R tends to a finite limit for m < n/2, grows like n^(1/4) at m = n/2, and grows exponentially for m > n/2; the note gives results for every regime, including m much larger than n, many with explicit constants. Two files: the working note as of 18 October 2026, and an addendum recording all later work (a full 1/n expansion with certified error bounds, the exact Stokes constant of the crossover function, explicit large-m constants, and corrections). Every result is labelled proved, certified, formal, checked, or conjecture. None has yet been checked line by line by the author or an outside referee; the work is under review. The results were derived with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) under the author's direction and tested against exact-arithmetic and interval-arithmetic scripts; agreement between AI systems was not treated as verification.
